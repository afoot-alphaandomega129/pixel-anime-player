## 🎧 How to Use

The player interface organizes your music collection. You control the experience through the main menu.

### Adding Music
Click the folder icon to select a directory on your computer. The player scans your music files and adds them to your library. It supports common formats like MP3 and WAV.

### Syncing Lyrics
The player detects lyrics if you provide a file with the same name as your music track in the same folder. Use files with the `.lrc` extension. If the player finds them, lyrics appear on screen as the song plays.

### Visual Effects
The beat-reactive dancers appear automatically when you play a track. You can choose different characters from the settings menu. Each character follows the tempo of your music using audio processing tools.

### Photo Pixelization
You can upload your own photos to the player. The software converts these images into pixel art style. Use these images as custom backgrounds for your music player. Access this feature through the "Visuals" tab.

## 💡 Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the player send my music to the internet?
The software works locally on your machine. It does not upload your music files to a server. 

### Can I use the player offline?
Yes. The software requires no internet connection for music playback or dancer animations.

### How do I remove the software?
Open your Windows Settings, go to Apps, find the player in the list, and select Uninstall. This removes the program from your hard drive.

### The dancer animation looks choppy. How do I fix this?
Close other programs that use many resources. The animations require a small amount of processing power. If your computer runs many heavy tasks at once, the motion of the pixel art might slow down.

### Why do some lyrics not sync?
The player looks for an `.lrc` file that matches the name of your music file exactly. Ensure the names match and that both files live in the same folder.

## 🛠 Managing Media Folders

The player keeps your music organized. If you add new songs to your computer folders, refresh the library view within the application settings. This prompts the player to scan for new entries so they appear in your list. 

Do not move music files while the player is open. Keep your files in stable locations on your hard drive to prevent errors during playback. If you move a file after adding it to the library, the player shows a "File Not Found" warning. Use the "Remove Missing Files" button in settings to clean your library list.
